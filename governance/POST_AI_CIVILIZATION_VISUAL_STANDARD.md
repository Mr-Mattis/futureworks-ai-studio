# Future Works AI Studio — Post-Human/AI Co-Design Visual Standard, v1

Owner creative feedback, 2026-11-17: V4 achieved convincing photography/material realism, but both samples read as modern U.S. military vehicles and do not communicate advanced scale or future civilization. **Do not approve V4-A or V4-B as final collection assets.** Use them only for technical calibration of tire treads, hardware, weathering and optics.

## Visual North Star

Design the built environment, tools, habitats and machines of a highly advanced human civilization after long-term creative and scientific collaboration with increasingly capable AI. Every design must feel like a genuine engineered artifact from that future, **not** a present-day military rover with an LED strip, generic retro-futurism or fantasy concept art. Show humane purpose, scientific maturity, plausible function and distinct visual identity.

## Enforceable production criteria

- **Function first.** Every machine has a clear civilian, scientific, ecological, medical, industrial or exploration mission. Show an identifiable subsystem performing that mission.
- **Silhouette uniqueness.** Each of the ten concepts must differ in primary geometry, mobility or deployment architecture, mass distribution and operating environment. Repeated quadrupeds, trucks, armored tank hulls and humanoid soldiers fail.
- **Advanced principles, visibly grounded.** Machine designs may exploit speculative adaptive materials, distributed intelligence, in-situ manufacturing, robotic self-assembly, field-based manipulation, biohybrid interfaces, swarm coordination, high-energy efficiency, optical sensing and autonomous repair. Label speculative mechanisms as speculative; no false implication of demonstrated real-world performance.
- **Scale proof.** Every full-vehicle or structure image needs human-scale or known-size reference: access hatch, handrail, work platform, maintenance drone, service robot, cargo pallet, structural joint, window or terrain feature. Specify approximate dimensions (m) in prompt and verify perspective.
- **Mechanical credibility.** Bearings, actuators, articulated joints, braces, cables and thermal systems connect logically and serve real loads. Visible fasteners, access panels, tolerances and maintenance paths should make sense.
- **Photographic materials.** Precision-machined alloys, ceramic-matrix composites, ultra-fine abrasion, selective oxidation, realistic micro-scratches, dirt in seams, varied roughness, wear appropriate to mission. No airbrushed painterly texture, uniform chrome, melted machinery, random illuminated decorative greebles.
- **Human-centered and nonmilitary.** Avoid camouflage, gun mounts, turret silhouettes, contemporary APC proportions, intimidating tactical paint/decals, battle scenes and insignia. Design for durable service, cooperation, exploration and infrastructure.
- **Camera direction.** Use validated MOV/ANG/FRM/OPT catalog IDs, a 5–10s motion plan, fixed physical orientation and motion continuity for Runway. Favor physically plausible orbit/dolly, macro detail reveals and wide scale establishing shots rather than arbitrary morphing.
- **Release boundaries.** New designs are local review candidates only. Owner's previous rights and external upload approval were exclusively for the first P04_GPU_001 S01–S03 images, not this future collection. No Runway spend or publication automatically authorized.

## Acceptance protocol

For each concept: generate at native photographic fidelity with verified model hashes, run actual image-space super-resolution to **1920×1080**, show individual 100% crops, evaluate engineering plausibility and nonmilitary novelty, reject fused geometry or missing scale cues. Owner approves exact image hashes individually. Retain source prompts, seeds, model/version/license, Comfy receipts, model hashes, and final bytes. Do not confuse 1080p output dimensions with native diffusion or true optically captured detail.

**Required owner input after initial representative samples:** select best future silhouette and material realism, before batch of ten. No paid services or production activation included in this plan.
